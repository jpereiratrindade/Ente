The question

What makes a changing system remain the same system?

ENTE investigates continuity not as sameness of code, process, memory or hardware, but as a verifiable relationship between genesis, history, authority, interpretation and transformation.

01
If the hardware changes, what exactly persists?
02
If the system changes its interpretation of the world, what preserves continuity?
03
If the process terminates, what exactly wakes up again?
04
If evidence becomes insufficient, how should action change without pretending uncertainty is certainty?
What ENTE preserves

Continuity is not assumed. It is constituted.

ENTE-0 operationalizes a constitution of explicit invariants. The website summarizes them here; the normative definition remains in the ENTE Constitution.

IDENTITY

One genesis. One distinguishable identity. No silent replacement by a second beginning.

CONTINUITY

Legitimate transformations remain verifiably connected to the state and trajectory that preceded them.

PROVENANCE

Material transformations retain evidence, causal relationships and historical justification.

AUTHORITY

Constitutive actions belong to a legitimate and auditable lineage of authority epochs.

EPISTEMIC COHERENCE

Observed, inferred, unknown, uncertain and contradictory are represented as distinct epistemic states.

RECOVERABILITY

History can reconstruct the trajectory of the ENTE from its genesis rather than inventing a new one.

A life, not a deployment

One continuous trajectory across change.

ENTE-0's vertical lifecycle experiment connects genesis, observation, epistemic perturbation, action suspension, material migration, process termination and cold recovery.

The system maintains an interpretation justified by the evidence available at that moment.

Case Lab 001 · KitKat analog

The system does not need to know it is a KitKat.

The experiment asks a more fundamental question: what should happen when the evidence no longer supports acting as though the environment were safely understood?

Conventional baseline

The catalogued obstacle classifier still sees no known obstacle blocking the frontal path.

front_path = CLEAR
near_wheel_motion = UNKNOWN
known_obstacle = FALSE
↓
DEPART
→

ENTE-mediated agent

The unknown near-wheel evidence weakens the interpretation under which departure was previously justified.

front_path = CLEAR
near_wheel_motion = UNKNOWN
RCC = WEAKENED
↓
RUNTIME_ASSURANCE = SAFE_HOLD

ENTE does not need to identify the cat. It needs to recognize that it no longer knows enough to act as if nothing changed.
